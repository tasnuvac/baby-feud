Rules

Game Setup

Two teams play five regular Baby Feud rounds. Enter both team names, then begin the game.

The TV/computer acts as the host screen. The host types the answers, controls the music, pauses the game, and manages overrides.

Face-Off Round

Each round begins with a face-off team. Teams do not need to pick their answer person during the loading screen.

Once the question appears, the team can discuss quickly and send one person up to the computer to type the answer.

If the answer is on the board, that team chooses whether to play the board or pass it to the other team.

Play / Pass

If a team chooses Play, they keep guessing answers on the board. If they choose Pass, the other team takes control of the board.

As the round is going, the team can choose who comes up to type for each turn. The person typing should enter the team’s final answer.

The bank grows as correct answers are found. The controlling team keeps going until they clear the board or get three strikes.

Strikes and Steal

A wrong answer gives the controlling team one strike. After three strikes, the other team gets a chance to steal.

If the stealing team gives a correct answer, they win the bank. If they miss, the controlling team keeps the bank.

Timer, Hints, and Music

Each regular round has a timer. The host can pause and resume the game if needed.

Hints appear after two strikes. They are meant to help without giving away the exact answer.

At one minute left, the timer audio starts. The answering music lowers slightly while the timer sound plays.

Host Override

After answers are revealed, the host can add override points if a team gave an answer that should count.

Override points must match one of the answer point values from that round. The host can add multiple overrides, then press Done.

Fast Money

Fast Money uses one player from each team. The team with the higher regular-round score goes first.

Both teams get time to choose their representative before Fast Money starts.

The second team cannot repeat the first team’s Fast Money answers. Fast Money points are added to each team’s score to decide the final winner.
